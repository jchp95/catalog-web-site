# Design Direction — V3

The design rule remains strict: **a prospect must not feel that each vertical is the same template with a new color.** Shared architecture remains invisible.

## LOCAL/SHOWROOM

Subject: a live sales instrument for U.S. local-business owners. Signature: overlapping live-deck entry plus a prospect personalization console. Palette: porcelain, ink, cobalt, signal red, cold mist. Type: Bricolage Grotesque + Instrument Serif + DM Mono.

## MIREYA

World: consultation, mirror arches, touch and quiet luxury. Signature: sculptural portrait/arch plus a booking sequence. Lavender and petal tones are contained to this vertical.

## APEX

World: inspection bay, surface measurement, coating and scanners. Signature: reactive top-view vehicle SVG and drag-based paint comparison. Technical cyan and tungsten black.

## BLACKLINE

World: barber chair hardware, queue board, sharp typography and shop-floor rhythm. Signature: a constructed barber chair hero and live wait-time selection. Hard charcoal, bone and oxidized orange.

## CASA FUEGO

World: live flame, chef counter, thermal gradients and plated food. Signature: dish selection changes a heat index and a constructed plate. Coal, ash, fire orange and ember gold.

## HARBOR

World: shoreline maps, architecture, advisory and property discovery. Signature: abstract cartography feeds into a live filter/save/tour workflow. Paper, blue-gray water and architectural ink.

## BRIGHTLINE

World: house plans, pipes, circuits, HVAC schematics and dispatch. Signature: blueprint system diagram plus guided service intake. White technical paper, navy and service blue.

## Selective 3D

The showroom hero and APEX each carry one real-time WebGL moment — a drag-to-explore photo gallery and a drag-to-inspect vehicle stage — chosen because both are natural "look at this from every angle" gestures, not because 3D was owed to every section. See `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#progressive-3d` for the engineering rules that keep them from ever being load-bearing.

## Rejected defaults

- one component template recolored six times;
- arbitrary glass cards;
- gradient blobs as a substitute for art direction;
- animations attached to every section;
- 3D/WebGL without a specific interaction it earns its place for;
- fake metrics presented as factual business claims outside demo context.
